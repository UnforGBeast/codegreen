FRESHWATER

An estimate of the freshwater used by the AI tokens your coding tools have logged on this computer. Nothing leaves your machine unless you switch on update checks.

Water rate

How it is calculated

weighted tokens = output x 1
                + input x 0.1
                + cache write x 0.1
                + cache read x 0.001
energy (Wh) = weighted tokens / 1000 x 0.6
water (mL)  = energy (Wh) x water rate

The token weights are judgment values, not sourced figures: cached tokens are about 98% of Claude Code volume, so counting them in full would swamp everything else. The 0.6 Wh per 1,000 tokens comes from Epoch AI's estimate of about 0.3 Wh per ChatGPT query, assuming a 500-token response.

Sources

  • Epoch AI, "How much energy does ChatGPT use?"
  • Li et al., "Making AI Less Thirsty", arXiv 2304.03271, version 5
  • Google's published per-prompt figures for a median Gemini text prompt

Low counts cooling water only. Default and High count cooling plus electricity water. The full methodology and its caveats are in docs/methodology.md in the project repository.

Tools
